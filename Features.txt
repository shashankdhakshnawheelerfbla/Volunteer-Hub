1. User Accounts and Authentication
- Email and password login/signup: Users can create accounts and securely access Volunteer Hub using their email and password.
- Google sign-in: Users can register or log in using their Google accounts.
- Microsoft sign-in: Users can authenticate using their Microsoft accounts.
- Apple sign-in: Users can authenticate using their Apple accounts.
- User profiles: Each user has a profile containing their personal information and volunteer activity.
- Session management: Keep users logged in securely and allow them to log out when finished.
2. Volunteer Opportunity Management
- Create opportunities: Users organizing volunteer events can publish new opportunities.
- Edit opportunities: Update descriptions, dates, times, locations, and other event information.
- Delete opportunities: Remove opportunities that are canceled or no longer available.
- Opportunity descriptions: Explain the responsibilities, requirements, and purpose of each event.
- Volunteer capacity: Set the maximum number of volunteers allowed at an event.
- Registered volunteer lists: View everyone who has signed up for a particular opportunity.
3. Volunteer Signup Management
- Browse opportunities: View available volunteer opportunities.
- Register for opportunities: Sign up to participate in an event.
- Cancel registrations: Withdraw from an opportunity when plans change.
- Available slots: Display how many volunteer positions remain.
- Prevent overbooking: Stop additional registrations when an event reaches capacity.
- Registration history: View previously registered opportunities.
4. Volunteer-Hour Tracking
- Submit service hours: Volunteers can enter hours they've completed at an event or organization.
- Service dates: Record when the volunteer work took place.
- Activity descriptions: Explain what work was completed.
- Quarter-hour increments: Hours must be entered in 0.25-hour intervals, equivalent to 15-minute intervals. For example, 30 minutes is 0.50 hours.
- Service-hour history: Keep a record of previously submitted volunteer activities.
- Total hours: Calculate the total number of submitted and approved service hours.
5. Volunteer-Hour Verification
- Review submissions: Coordinators can examine submitted volunteer-hour records.
- Approve submissions: Confirm that the reported service hours are accurate.
- Reject submissions: Reject inaccurate or incomplete records.
- Submission statuses: Display whether a submission is pending, approved, or rejected.
- Verification history: Maintain records of which submissions were approved or rejected and when.
6. Printable Volunteer-Hour Reports
- Generate reports: Compile a volunteer's service-hour information into a structured report.
- Print reports: Allow users to print their volunteer-hour records.
- PDF downloads: Generate downloadable PDF copies.
- Student information: Include the volunteer's name and relevant identification information.
- Service details: Include activity descriptions, dates, and hours completed.
- Verified totals: Clearly distinguish approved hours from pending submissions.
- Coordinator information: Include relevant event coordinator or adult sponsor information.
7. Email Functionality
- Email reports: Send completed volunteer-hour reports directly through email.
- Student email delivery: Allow volunteers to receive copies of their reports.
- Adult sponsor copies: Allow reports to be sent to an adult sponsor's email address.
- Verified-hour summaries: Include the total number of approved service hours.
- Report attachments: Include PDF copies of the reports when emailing them.
8. Database and Account Integration
- Main volunteer database: Store volunteer profiles, opportunities, registrations, and service-hour records.
- Separate social-account database: Store information connecting external authentication providers to user accounts.
- Google account connections: Associate Google accounts with Volunteer Hub accounts.
- Microsoft account connections: Associate Microsoft accounts with Volunteer Hub accounts.
- Apple account connections: Associate Apple accounts with Volunteer Hub accounts.
- Backend integration: Connect the frontend to the Python backend and Cloudflare database so information is saved and retrieved correctly.
9. Dashboard and User Interface
- Excel-inspired interface: Use organized rows, columns, and tables to make information easy to manage.
- Volunteer dashboard: Display personal information, registrations, service-hour history, and totals.
- Opportunity dashboard: Display available and published volunteer opportunities.
- Hours dashboard: Display submitted, approved, rejected, and pending service hours.
- Organized records: Make volunteer information easy to search, sort, and review.
- Unique branding: Create original branding, colors, logo, and layout rather than copying SignUpGenius or Excel.
